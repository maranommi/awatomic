About

Awat Serdashti is an Oslo-based artist with a background in graffiti. Originally from Kurdistan and raised in Kongsberg, he has used the city as a sketchbook since the late 1990s, developing a raw, spontaneous and poetic visual language of his own.

Workshops

Awat Serdashti offers graffiti and street art workshops for children and young people.

The workshops are based on graffiti, colour, form and visual expression, combining practical exercises with an open and playful approach to making art.

Workshops can be adapted to different ages, group sizes and settings, and have been held at schools, youth clubs, art organisations and cultural events.

Previous workshops include projects at Lakkegata School in Oslo, Kongsberg Kunstforening, Midtgaard Fritidsklubb in Ås and Napern 1916 in Sandefjord.

For workshop enquiries and collaborations, please get in touch.
