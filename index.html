About

Awat Serdashti is an Oslo-based artist with a background in graffiti and street culture. Originally from Kurdistan and raised in Kongsberg, he has used the city as a sketchbook since the late 1990s, developing a raw, spontaneous and poetic visual language of his own.

His practice grew out of years in graffiti and street culture, but has developed further into painting, sculpture and public art. He works intuitively and physically, often on a large scale and with a high degree of intensity.

Forms emerge spontaneously, while carrying clear references to the body, movement and energy. The female body and the ways in which people move have been recurring sources of inspiration. His visual signature lies not only in the forms themselves, but also in his use of colour, combining vivid tones with reflective metallic paint.

He is largely self-taught, with additional studies at Prosjektskolen in Oslo.
